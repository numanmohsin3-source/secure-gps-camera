Privacy Policy - Secure GPS Map Camera

Last updated: November 2026

Secure GPS Map Camera is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This privacy policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our application.

1. 100% Offline Application & Data Collection

Secure GPS Map Camera is an offline utility app. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, location coordinates, captured photos, or device identifiers to any remote server or third party.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely to take photos with live GPS metadata overlays. Photos remain strictly on your local device.
  • Precise Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used strictly on your device to display live GPS coordinates, accuracy, altitude, and address on the camera viewfinder and watermark overlay. Location data is never transmitted.
  • Storage / Media: Used to save photos locally in your private encrypted vault and public photo gallery upon your request.
  • Biometrics: Used solely by Android OS to authenticate your identity to unlock your local encrypted vault. Biometric data never leaves Android KeyStore hardware.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Our app does not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks. No user behavior is monitored or tracked.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal information from children or adults alike.
